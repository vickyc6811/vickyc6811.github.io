SEP11 Freedom Project

This is my SEP11 Freedom Project

The “Freedom Project” for SEP11 is all about making a typing game to train people to type fast. The purpose of this blog is to show the process of planning and creating my Speed Typing Game with Javascript and p5js.

For my project, I am making a Speed Typing Game. This blog will walk through my process of independently studying Javascript and our tool, p5js, in order to make a fun interacting game that trains people’s typing.

Challenges: Some challenges I had was researching useful resources to help me learn my tool, p5js, alongside Javscript. I had to do a lot of Googling and watch tutorial videos to help me learn how to make my Speed Typing Game. Some takeaways were time management, it's much better to plan out a schedule on what days you're going to working on the Freedom Project. It's good to not cram everything last minute, this helps managing stress. My next steps are improving the Speed Typing Game as it'll be much better with more words added.
